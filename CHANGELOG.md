- Truth or Dare mode with slot-machine roll animation
- Random Question mode with swipeable pager organized by theme
- In-app guide screen accessible from each feature
- Questions fetched from Firebase Hosting and cached in Room
- Offline fallback to bundled `assets/question.json`
- Player tracking with turn rotation and score counting
